How it works

Hand tracking

MediaPipe detects both hands and 21 joints on each, up to 30 times a second, inside the browser.

Shape classification

A graph-attention network (DH-GAT) processes both hands jointly, so that contact such as a fingertip on the other palm is represented explicitly.

Word decoding

A Conformer sequence model with CTC decoding converts the stream of hand shapes into letters, including letters signed in quick succession.

Accuracy depends on the camera and the signer. The results page reports the measured figures, including an evaluation on a camera not seen in training, and the limitations of the system.
