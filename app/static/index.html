How it works

Track the hands

MediaPipe finds both hands and 21 joints on each, 30 times a second, right in the browser.

Understand the shape

A graph-attention network looks at both hands together, so “fingertip on the other palm” is something it can actually see.

Spell the word

A Conformer sequence model turns the stream of hand shapes into letters, even when they run together.
